Your privacy

PrivacyPolicy

Plain English, no surprises. This covers SarahTalks.tv — the website and its booking form. The Sarah's BB Bible app has its own detailed policy, linked at the bottom.

Last updated August 9, 2026

The short version

This site is a simple marketing page. We don't sell your data, we don't run advertising trackers, and the only information we collect is what you choose to type into the booking form so we can reply to you.

What we collect

Only what you submit through the Book Sarah inquiry form: your name, the company or podcast you represent, your email address, the type of opportunity, and any details you add. Nothing else on this site asks for personal information.

How we use it

Booking inquiries are sent to Kinetic Management, who manages Sarah's bookings, so we can respond to you. We use your details only to reply and to arrange the opportunity you asked about. We don't add you to a marketing list and we don't share your inquiry with anyone outside of handling your request.

How it's handled

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form submissions are delivered by email through our processor (Resend) to Kinetic Management. These providers process the data only to deliver the site and your message. We keep inquiry emails only as long as needed to follow up with you.

Cookies & tracking

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Your light/dark theme choice is stored on your own device only. That's it.

Your choices

Want to see, correct, or delete an inquiry you sent us? Email craig@kinetic-mgmt.com and we'll take care of it.

Contact
