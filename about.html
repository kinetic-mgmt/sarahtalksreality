About Sarah

Started with a laptop& a lot to say

Sarah has been in the reality TV world for over a decade — and she didn't start with a platform. She started with a laptop, a JokersUpdates.com account, and way too much to say about Big Brother.

From there she built something real. She grew into one of TikTok's most recognized reality TV voices, launched Beyond the Edit — an interview podcast where she sits down with the people you watched and hears the stories that never made the edit — and, alongside her business partner and friend Craig Rogers of Kinetic Management, co-created the BB Bible app, a live spoiler and superfan community that thousands of fans use every single season.

She also hosts watch parties. Not the kind where you sit quietly and watch TV — the kind where you show up, meet your favorite cast members in person, win prizes, and leave with new friends who are just as unhinged about this stuff as you are. She's hosted across Texas, Nashville, and she's not stopping there.

Live, loud, and always with receipts.

She covers Big Brother, Survivor, The Traitors, Love Island, Real Housewives, and more — and she's been doing this long enough to know the game inside and out. She is not above a hot take.
